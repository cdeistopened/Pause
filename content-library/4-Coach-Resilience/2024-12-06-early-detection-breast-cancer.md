## Caption

Did you know a mammogram might not be enough?
My wife Jolie had clear mammograms for years, but it wasn’t until she had an ultrasound that her breast cancer was detected. Early detection saves lives—ask your doctor what additional tests you may need. Don’t wait to advocate for yourself—it could make all the difference.
