## Caption

How we talk to ourselves—both in our minds and out loud—shapes our mental and physical well-being. Positive self-talk can boost our mood, while negative self-talk can lead to anxiety and depression. Remember, building yourself up is key to good mental health. Start today: What are you telling yourself?
