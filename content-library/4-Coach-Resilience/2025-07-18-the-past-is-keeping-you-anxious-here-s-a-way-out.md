## Transcript

I'm an 86-year-old clinical psychologist, and I'm here to tell you the past is a trap. It's a quagmire. It is nothing but trouble. There is very little benefit, but there is some. We can learn from the past if we look at it carefully, and then we can improve in the future. But that is the only purpose of the past to review and learn. Anything else, recriminations, you should have done this. You could have done it better. Why didn't you do this? All that kind of stuff. Waste of time. Forget about it. Stay in the present. Use the past to learn something from. But when you do, you stand back. You don't get emotionally involved and start to give yourself a hard time for mistakes you made in the past. We all make mistakes. We just need to move on, do better in the future, correct the mistakes, and have a wonderful day. Do not let the past get in your way. Push it out of your mind. Learn how to take control of your mind.
