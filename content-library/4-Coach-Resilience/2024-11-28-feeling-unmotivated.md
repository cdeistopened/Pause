## Caption

Feeling unmotivated? Try this simple trick
At 85, I don’t always feel motivated—but I’ve learned how to coach myself out loud. I talk to myself like I would a good friend: ‘Get up, Miller. You’ve got this!’ It’s not just about what you say—it’s the act of speaking it out loud that gets you moving. Whether it’s grabbing a book, going for a walk, or finishing a task, that little voice can make all the difference.
