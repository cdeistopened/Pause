## Caption

What if I told you there’s no such thing as laziness?
It’s just a label we use to criticize inactivity. But maybe, just maybe, your body knows better—it’s asking for rest and recovery. Instead of fighting it, lean into it. Be kind to yourself. Slow down. And when you’re ready, ease back into activity. 
Rest is not a flaw; it’s part of your healing.
