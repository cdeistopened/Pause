## Caption

After a Winnebago crushed both my legs, I refused to accept the wheelchair as my future.
I worked hard, maintained my attitude, and eventually completed a triathlon. Attitude is everything. Set it, check it, and choose it every day.

## Transcript

Some years ago, I was hit head-on by a Winnebago recreational vehicle. It crushed both my legs. I had to fight my way out of having both my legs amputated. But what did I do? I woke up in intensive care after a 15 and a half hour surgery, and the first thing I did was call a bunch of friends and family, invited them to the ICU, and we had a party. I maintained my attitude, and I filled myself with golden light. I was told I'd never get out of the wheelchair, but I didn't accept that. I started doing laps with the wheelchair while I was still in the hospital. I said before two years are up, I'm going to do a triathlon, and I did. I got out of the wheelchair after six months, and I completed a triathlon within two years. I used crutches for running, I did, but I got through it. What's it about? It's about attitude is everything, folks. I maintain a positive attitude by checking in with my attitude many times during the day, and if I feel my attitude slipping, I raise it. I make myself have a good attitude. I direct my attitude. I choose the attitude that I want rather than the attitude that's coming at me. You can do the same. Choose your own attitude many times a day, and live in bliss, grace, and gratitude.
