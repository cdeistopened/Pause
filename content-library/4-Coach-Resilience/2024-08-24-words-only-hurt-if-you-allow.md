## Caption

How to Overcome Hurtful Words

## Transcript

People ask me, how do you move on after you've been hurt by something somebody has said or done to you? Well, to begin with, I think of what my grandmother taught me. Sticks and stones can hurt your bones, but names will never harm you. And there's a lot of truth in that. If somebody hits you with a stick or a stone, that hurts, and that can do damage to your body. But when somebody calls us a name, that in and of itself does not hurt us. We have to do something inside of ourselves in order for those words to hurt us. Otherwise, we can leave those words with the person. We can say, all right, that's the kind of person that person wants to be, a person who speaks vilely to other people, who attacks or aggressors, or who says nasty things or does name calling. But that's them. That's not me. I don't need to take that stuff in. So the first thing you want to do with regard to getting hurt from others is really consider how much you want to let words, mere words, grunts, sounds that come into our ears and we decode, how much do you want to let them hurt you? This is a decision you can make.
