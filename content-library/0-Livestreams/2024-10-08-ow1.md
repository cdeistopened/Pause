## Caption

Letting go of the past and staying out of the future—it's a lesson I keep coming back to because it’s essential for living a happy life.
My wife’s recent diagnosis with breast cancer has reminded us both of the importance of this. We don’t know what the future holds—more chemo, radiation, maybe more surgery—but we’re not living in the 'what ifs.' Instead, we’re focused on the present, appreciating each other, and expressing gratitude for the here and now.
