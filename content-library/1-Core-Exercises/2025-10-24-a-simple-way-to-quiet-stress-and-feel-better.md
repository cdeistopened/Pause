## Transcript

I've talked about the relationship between stress, negativity, and cortisol, the stress hormone. Raising stress and raising cortisol creates a lot of psychological and physiological problems. What's the flip side of that? Expressing gratitude. It turns out that saying nice things to yourself, expressing gratitude to yourself, or to other people, lowers your cortisol level, increases your immune system, helps with anxiety and depression, a myriad of things simply by expressing gratitude to yourself and other people.
