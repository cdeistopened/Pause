I'm an 85 year old clinical psychologist. During the pandemic, my gym was closed. I didn't get to work out. I lost a lot of muscle mass. Why is it important to stand up straight? Because when you stand up straight, you're aligning your body, you're aligning all the vertebrae and the skeletal system, you're creating balance. When you create balance, the entire system knows it. It affects your psyche as well as your physiology. You feel upright, you act upright. There's a connection between the mind and the body and we must remember that. I mean, it's more than a connection, it's really all one. We're not a mind and a body, we're a mind body. That's what we are, mind, body, spirit. And the posture is very important because it affects how we feel. So practice slouching, practice standing up straight, raise your awareness, raise how you feel in the world, change your sense of optimism.
