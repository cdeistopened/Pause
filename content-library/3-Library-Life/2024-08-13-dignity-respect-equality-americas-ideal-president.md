## Caption

My Vision for America's Presidency

## Transcript

I want a president who will treat all people with dignity, respect, kindness, and love. I want a president who has a friendly face and an honest, warm smile. I want a president who treats all people with equal dignity and respect, whether it's the house cleaner or the speaker of the house. I want a president who believes that all citizens are entitled to health care, education, shelter, food, and warmth. I want a president who believes that all women are in charge of their own bodies and no one else is in charge of their bodies. I want a president who understands that except for Native Americans, we are all immigrants, and that's what makes America the powerhouse and the beacon of light to the world, because we are representative of every country in the world. That's who we are. I want a president who understands America's experiment with democracy and republic, a democracy where every person has a vote, a republic where every person is equal before the rule of law.
