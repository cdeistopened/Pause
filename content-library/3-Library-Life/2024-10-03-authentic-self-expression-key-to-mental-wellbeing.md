## Caption

After 64 years as a clinical psychologist, here’s a truth I’ve learned: your self-image is deeply rooted in your mind and nervous system. The real you can only emerge when you feel safe enough to see yourself and safe enough to show others.
Unfortunately, our society often pressures us with shame, guilt, and blame, forcing us to shrink into a version of ourselves that pleases others but neglects our true self.
Your perception of yourself shapes how others perceive you. But remember—you have the power to change this from within. It’s about positive thinking, taking control of your mind, and living authentically in the present moment.
