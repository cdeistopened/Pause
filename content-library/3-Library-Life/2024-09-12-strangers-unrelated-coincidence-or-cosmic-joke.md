## Caption

Your mind is like a garden: negative self-talk? That’s the weeds. Positive affirmations? Those are the seeds.
Just as you wouldn’t let your garden be overrun with weeds, don’t let negative thoughts run wild. Every time you practice controlling your mind—whether through breathing, counting, or focusing on the positive—you’re planting seeds that strengthen your mental health. It’s all connected: your mind, body, and spirit. Remember, you are the boss of your thoughts, and that can transform everything.
What seeds are you planting today?
