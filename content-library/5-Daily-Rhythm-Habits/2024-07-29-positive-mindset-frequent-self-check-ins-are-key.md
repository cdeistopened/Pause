## Transcript

I'm an 85 year old clinical psychologist with 64 years of practice experience. People often ask me, how do you stay so positive at your age? How do you stay positive at any age? And the answer is reminders. I remind myself to stay positive. I remind myself frequently during the day. I check in on my mood. This is essential. Often we're going through life so fast, we're doing so much. We don't check in on ourselves. It's really important to take a moment, 30 seconds, sit down, close your eyes, check in on yourself. Ask yourself, how am I doing? How am I feeling? And if you're feeling anything other than the way you want to feel, then set your attitude. Set the attitude for the attitude you want and continue in that attitude. And then maybe an hour or two later, do it again. Check in on yourself. This is the way to stay positive and to maintain mental control by checking in on ourselves and then deciding how we want to think and feel. The check in is essential and it doesn't take very long. You can do it in 30 seconds or a minute, but don't let the days rush by without checking in with yourself.
