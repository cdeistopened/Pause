## Caption

Your Mind is a Tool—Not the Boss. Stop letting It control YOU.
Our mind likes to believe it’s in control, but the truth is, we are the boss. The mind is just a tool—like a computer or any other device we use. When it tries to run the show with unwanted thoughts, take charge. Practice counting, shift your focus, and remind yourself that you decide what occupies your mind. Don’t let it win—choose beautiful thoughts.
