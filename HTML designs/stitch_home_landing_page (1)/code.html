Aspirin

Aspirin (Acetylsalicylic acid)

Supports mitochondrial respiration and reduces systemic inflammation by uncoupling oxidative phosphorylation.

Mechanism of Action

Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) acts primarily as an anti-inflammatory agent by inhibiting the production of prostaglandins. In the context of bioenergetics, its value lies in its ability to safely uncouple mitochondrial respiration, thereby increasing metabolic rate and carbon dioxide production.

Furthermore, its antagonism of estrogen synthesis makes it a critical tool for managing conditions exacerbated by estrogen dominance.
