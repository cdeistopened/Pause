Carbon Dioxide (CO2)

Pro-Metabolic

The essential protective factor of the organism. CO2 is not merely a waste product but a fundamental regulator of cell excitation and energy production. High levels promote efficient respiration and calmness.

Category: Physiology
arrow_forward

Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids (PUFA)

Anti-Metabolic

Unstable fats that suppress thyroid function, inhibit proteolytic enzymes, and promote oxidative stress. Found in seed oils, fish oil, and nuts. They are considered a primary cause of degeneration and aging.

Category: Nutrition
arrow_forward

Thyroid Hormone

Pro-Metabolic

The master regulator of metabolism. Proper thyroid function ensures efficient energy production (oxidative phosphorylation), high body temperature, and protection against stress hormones.

Category: Hormones
arrow_forward

Serotonin

Anti-Metabolic

Often mislabeled as a "happy hormone," serotonin is actually a mediator of stress, inflammation, and hibernation. Excess serotonin lowers metabolic rate and promotes fibrosis.

Category: Hormones
arrow_forward

Carrot Salad

Therapeutic

A simple daily salad of raw shredded carrots, vinegar, coconut oil, and salt. Used to reduce intestinal endotoxin and help excrete estrogen from the body.

Category: Nutrition
arrow_forward

Aspirin

Pro-Metabolic

A metabolic protectant that inhibits prostaglandin synthesis, reduces inflammation, lowers stress, and can uncouple oxidative phosphorylation to increase heat production.

Category: Substances
arrow_forward

Estrogen

Anti-Metabolic

The shock hormone. While essential for reproduction, excess estrogen promotes water retention, hypoxia, fibrosis, and interferes with thyroid function.

Category: Hormones
