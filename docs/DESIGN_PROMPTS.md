## PROMPT 1: Pause Screen (Home)

```
Design a mobile app home screen for a mindfulness app called "The Pause."

LAYOUT:
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A), full bleed
- Small streak counter in top-right corner: "47" with flame icon, subtle pill container
- Large golden orb dominates the center (the main interactive element)
- Exercise selector as TEXT LABELS arranged in a circle AROUND the orb
- Subtle instruction text below the orb
- Standard bottom tab navigation bar (5 tabs)

THE GOLDEN ORB (Hero Element):
- Large circular element, approximately 180-200px diameter, perfectly centered
- Radial gradient from golden amber (#D4A853) at edges to soft gold/white (#F5DEB3) at center
- Soft golden glow/halo radiating outward (rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.3))
- Luminous center point that appears to emit light
- Suggests gentle pulsing animation through the glow effect

EXERCISE SELECTOR (Orbital Ring):
- 4 text labels positioned in a circle around the orb (like compass points)
- Top: "BREATH"
- Right: "LIGHT"
- Bottom: "COUNT"
- Left: "RELAX"
- The labels orbit the orb at roughly 60px distance from orb edge
- Typography: All caps, 12pt, letter-spacing expanded
- Selected state: Golden text (#D4A853), slightly larger (14pt)
- Unselected state: Muted gray (#5A5A5A)
- A subtle thin arc or ring connects the labels (very faint, like a constellation line)
- Premium exercises (RELAX) show tiny lock icon (🔒) next to label, same muted color
- NO emoji icons, NO buttons, NO cards - just clean typography

INSTRUCTION TEXT:
- Below the orbital selector area: "Hold to begin" in muted gray (#8A8A8A), 14pt
- Below that: "Ready when you are." in italics, more muted (#5A5A5A)

BOTTOM NAVIGATION BAR:
- Standard iOS tab bar at bottom
- 5 tabs: Library (book icon), Coach (mic icon), [ORB] (center, elevated), Habits (checkmark), Settings (gear)
- Center tab is a small golden orb icon, slightly raised/prominent
- Dark background matching app (#0A0E1A or slightly lighter)
- Active tab: golden, Inactive tabs: gray

STYLE:
- Spiritual and minimal - like a meditation mandala or astrolabe
- The orb is sacred, the selector orbits it like planets around a sun
- No clutter, no buttons, no cards - pure typography and light
- Premium, calm, intentional
- Feels like a ritual tool, not an app

REFERENCE: Think luxury watch face meets spiritual compass. The orb is the sun, exercises are constellations around it. Clean like Braun design, warm like candlelight.
```

## PROMPT 2: Active Pause Session

```
Design a mobile app screen showing an active meditation/breathing session in progress.

LAYOUT:
- Full screen experience, completely immersive
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A) with subtle golden gradient emanating from center
- The golden orb is now expanded and dominant, filling much of the screen
- Minimal UI - only the orb and a subtle progress indicator

THE EXPANDED ORB:
- Much larger now - approximately 280px diameter
- Same golden gradient (#D4A853 to #F5DEB3) but more luminous
- Strong golden glow effect radiating outward
- The orb should appear to "breathe" - show it at a slightly expanded state
- Audio waveform visualization integrated into the orb (subtle concentric rings that pulse)

BODY SILHOUETTE (OPTIONAL ELEMENT):
- Abstract human figure silhouette behind/within the orb
- The silhouette is being "filled with golden light" from bottom to top
- Currently showing about 60% filled (representing session progress)
- The unfilled portion is a very dark navy, the filled portion glows golden

PROGRESS INDICATOR:
- Very subtle circular progress ring around the outer edge of the orb
- Thin line (2px) showing how much of the 90-second session has elapsed
- Golden color (#D4A853) on dark background

TIMING:
- Small text at bottom: "0:47" showing elapsed time, very subtle

STYLE:
- Deeply immersive and calming
- The screen should feel like you're looking into warm golden light
- No distracting elements - the user's focus is entirely on the orb
- Premium, almost spiritual aesthetic
- The golden light should feel healing and energizing

REFERENCE: Like looking into a sunrise, or a warm glowing ember. Peaceful and mesmerizing.
```

## PROMPT 3: Intention Screen (Post-Pause)

```
Design a mobile app screen for capturing a user's intention after completing a meditation.

LAYOUT:
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A)
- Centered content with generous padding
- Single-purpose screen: capture one thought

HEADER:
- Question prompt at top-center: "What will you carry forward?"
- Typography: Light weight, 24pt, off-white (#F0F0F0)
- Generous top margin (about 30% down from top of screen)

TEXT INPUT:
- Single-line text input field
- Dark background (#1A1F2E) with subtle border
- Rounded corners (12px radius)
- Placeholder text: "Your intention..." in muted gray
- When active, subtle golden border glow
- Full width with 20px horizontal margins

BUTTONS:
- Primary button below input: "Done"
- Golden background (#D4A853), dark text (#0A0E1A)
- Rounded pill shape, generous padding
- Below primary button: "or skip →" as subtle text link in muted gray

KEYBOARD:
- Show iOS keyboard at bottom of screen (dark theme keyboard)
- The layout should account for keyboard being visible

STYLE:
- Calm and focused
- Single clear action
- No distractions
- The golden "Done" button is the only color accent
- Feels like a moment of reflection

REFERENCE: Like a journal entry prompt. Simple, clean, contemplative.
```

## PROMPT 4: Habits/Streak Screen with Vessel Visualization

```
Design a mobile app habits tracking screen with a unique "vessel of light" progress visualization.

LAYOUT:
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A)
- Header: "Today's Practice" with date "Dec 16" on the right
- Main feature: A glowing vessel/chalice showing accumulated progress
- Below: Individual habit cards
- Bottom: Stats summary

THE VESSEL VISUALIZATION (HERO ELEMENT):
- Centered chalice/vessel shape, approximately 200px tall
- Elegant curved silhouette like a wine glass or ceremonial cup
- The vessel is partially filled with glowing golden liquid light
- Fill level represents daily progress (show about 65% full)
- The golden liquid (#D4A853) glows and shimmers
- Unfilled portion shows vessel outline in subtle dark gray
- Soft golden particles or sparkles floating up from the liquid
- Radiating glow around the filled portion

VESSEL CONTEXT:
- Small text below vessel: "Today's light: 65%"
- Or: "6 of 9 practices complete"

HABIT CARDS (below vessel):
- 3 cards stacked vertically
- Each card: dark background (#1A1F2E), rounded corners

Card 1 - Breathing:
- Left: 🌬️ emoji
- Middle: "Breathing" title, "3 of 10 today" subtitle
- Right: Small circular progress ring (30% filled, golden)

Card 2 - Golden Light:
- Left: ✨ emoji
- Middle: "Golden Light" title, "Completed" subtitle
- Right: Checkmark in golden circle (completed state)

Card 3 - Hydration:
- Left: 💧 emoji
- Middle: "Hydration" title, "Tap to log" subtitle
- Right: Empty circle outline (not started)

STATS BAR (bottom):
- Horizontal section with two stats
- Left: "🔥 12" with "day streak" label
- Right: "📊 147" with "total pauses" label
- Divided by subtle vertical line

STYLE:
- The vessel is the emotional center of the screen
- It should feel rewarding to fill it up
- Premium and unique - not a typical progress bar
- The golden light feels like accumulating something valuable
- Motivating without being gamified

REFERENCE: Like filling a golden chalice with liquid sunlight. Sacred and meaningful.
```

## PROMPT 5: Coach Screen (Premium Feature)

```
Design a mobile app AI voice coach interface for a mindfulness app.

LAYOUT:
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A)
- Header: "Coach" title on left, purple "PREMIUM" badge on right
- Central avatar/presence area
- Chat-style conversation area
- Input bar at bottom with voice option

DR. MILLER AVATAR AREA:
- Circular container, approximately 120px diameter
- Could be a photo placeholder or abstract representation
- Subtle golden ring border (#D4A853)
- Below avatar: Quote in italics: "What's on your mind today?"
- The quote appears in off-white, centered

CONVERSATION AREA:
- Space for chat bubbles (currently empty/minimal for first visit)
- If showing a message: Assistant messages on left (dark gray background)
- User messages would be on right (golden tint background)

INPUT BAR:
- Fixed at bottom above keyboard area
- Dark background pill shape (#1A1F2E)
- Left side: Microphone icon button (golden color when active)
- Right side: Text input "Type or speak..."
- The mic button should look tappable and prominent

QUICK PROMPTS:
- Row of 3 suggestion chips above input bar
- "I'm anxious" | "Help me focus" | "What should I do?"
- Small rounded pills, dark background, muted text
- Tappable conversation starters

STYLE:
- Feels like talking to a wise mentor
- Warm and approachable
- Voice-first design (mic button is prominent)
- Not clinical - personal and supportive
- Premium feature aesthetic (the purple badge signals exclusivity)

REFERENCE: Like a high-end AI assistant interface, but warmer. Think therapist's office meets modern chat UI.
```

## PROMPT 6: Upgrade/Paywall Screen

```
Design a mobile app premium upgrade screen for a mindfulness app.

LAYOUT:
- Dark navy background (#0A0E1A)
- Centered content promoting premium features
- Clear pricing and call-to-action

HERO SECTION:
- Large microphone icon or voice waveform graphic at top
- Rendered in golden gradient (#D4A853 to #F5DEB3)
- Soft glow effect around it
- Below: "Voice Coaching" as main headline, 28pt, off-white

VALUE PROPOSITION:
- Subheadline: "Get personalized guidance from Dr. Miller's AI assistant"
- Muted gray color, centered, 16pt

FEATURE LIST:
- 3-4 bullet points with checkmarks
- "✓ Ask questions about your practice"
- "✓ Get exercise recommendations"
- "✓ Receive personalized check-ins"
- Golden checkmarks, off-white text
- Left-aligned with generous spacing

CTA BUTTON:
- Large pill-shaped button: "Upgrade to Premium"
- Golden background (#D4A853), dark text
- Full width with margins
- Prominent and inviting

PRICING:
- Below button: "$4.99/month or $39.99/year"
- Muted gray, smaller text
- Optional: "7-day free trial" badge

DISMISS OPTION:
- Subtle "Maybe later" text link at very bottom
- Very muted, doesn't compete with CTA

STYLE:
- Aspirational but not pushy
- The golden elements feel premium and desirable
- Clear value communication
- Respects the user's choice (easy to dismiss)
- Doesn't feel like a hard sell

REFERENCE: Apple's subscription prompts - elegant, clear value, easy decision.
```

## Design System Reference

Include these specs with any prompt for consistency:

```
COLORS:
- Background: #0A0E1A (deep navy)
- Background Light: #1A1F2E (cards, inputs)
- Primary: #D4A853 (golden amber)
- Primary Light: #F5DEB3 (soft gold)
- Text Primary: #F0F0F0 (off-white)
- Text Secondary: #8A8A8A (muted gray)
- Text Muted: #5A5A5A (hints)
- Premium: #9333EA (purple)
- Success: #10B981 (green)

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Headlines: SF Pro Display / 24-28pt / Light weight
- Body: SF Pro Text / 16pt / Regular
- Labels: SF Pro Text / 12-14pt / Medium
- Tracking: Slightly expanded for calm feel

SHAPES:
- Border radius: 12px for cards, 25px for buttons/pills
- Consistent 20px horizontal margins
- Generous vertical spacing

EFFECTS:
- Golden glow: box-shadow with rgba(212, 168, 83, 0.3)
- Subtle shadows on cards
- No harsh edges

PLATFORM:
- iOS-first design
- Safe areas for notch and home indicator
- 390 x 844px frame (iPhone 14 Pro)
```
