# Foreword: The Gift of Accumulated Intelligence

I am 86 years old. I have spent 65 of those years in clinical practice, sitting in a chair and listening to the secrets, the sorrows, and the triumphs of thousands of human beings. I have witnessed the darkest corners of institutional cruelty and the brightest moments of individual recovery.

I have spent a lifetime gathering tools.

Biological intelligence is an accumulation. Every patient I’ve helped, every trauma I’ve survived—including being crushed by a Winnebago on the Pacific Coast Highway—has left behind a deposit of data. This data has refined itself into a simple, high-performance system for staying sane in a world that is increasingly chaotic.

I call this my **Accumulated Intelligence**.

My goal for the last several years has been to "Open Source" this intelligence. I want these tools to be accessible to everyone, regardless of their ability to pay for a 50-minute therapy session. I want these protocols to be available to you when you are in your car, in your boardroom, or in your bed at 3:00 AM.

To achieve this, I have partnered with a new kind of technology: **Accumulated Intelligence Technology**.

The book you are holding is a distillation of my lifetime of training. It is the static manual. However, because a book cannot answer you when you are in a crisis, we have created an extension of this intelligence in the form of the **PAUSE App**.

The technology behind this app has been trained on my life’s work—my books, my transcripts, my lectures, and my clinical protocols. It allows for a **Dynamic Response**. If you tell the app you are feeling anxious, it will respond in my voice, using the exact protocols you are about to learn in these pages. It is a way for my accumulated experience to live and respond even when I am not in the room with you.

This is the future of mental health: the convergence of six decades of human wisdom with the power of modern intelligent systems. 

This book provides the framework. The app provides the saturation. Together, they offer you the opportunity to take command of your own biology. 

Good health is worth fighting for. It is the foundation for life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.

Let's begin the distillation.

— Dr. Richard Louis Miller
   *Fort Bragg, California*
