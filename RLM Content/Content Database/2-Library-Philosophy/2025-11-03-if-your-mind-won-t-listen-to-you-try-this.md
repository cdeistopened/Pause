## Caption

If your mind won't listen to you, TRY this.

## Transcript

So many people suffer from minds being out of control. Who is in charge? The answer is we are in charge, but it takes practice. I graduated with a master's and a PhD and then postdoctoral work. Nobody taught me how to control my mind. It takes daily practice for 30 to 60 or 90 seconds, three or four times a day. That means sitting down. quietly, looking inside and telling your mind what to do. You tell your mind what to do three or four or five times a day, after a while, your mind gets the message and it starts to listen, and it starts to do what you wanted to do.
