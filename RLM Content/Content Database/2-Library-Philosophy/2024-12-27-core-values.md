## Caption

What guides your life? Have you defined your core values?
Your core values shape your life, whether you realize it or not. Take a few minutes to write them down. What are your deal breakers? What truly matters to you? These values are your compass. Reflect on them, carry them with you, and live by them—they’re your guide to an authentic life.
