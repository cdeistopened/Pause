## Caption

Do you react—or respond? The difference can change everything.
Reactions are automatic, like a reflex. Responses, though, are intentional—they’re chosen. The next time someone says or does something that triggers you, pause. Take a moment to consider. Choose your words and actions deliberately.
Responding allows you to align with the kind of person you truly want to be. Practice this and watch how it transforms your relationships and your inner peace.
How do you handle moments that test your patience?

I'm Dr. Richard Lewis Miller, and the topic for today is reaction and response. A reaction, somebody says something or does something and we react almost like robots, fast, stimulus, response, automatic, very little thinking, very little choosing, very few options.
