## Caption

Your mind is a tool—not the boss.
Our mind wants to run the show, filling our heads with thoughts about the past, worries about the future, and criticisms we don’t need. But here’s the truth: you are the boss. With just 60-90 seconds of practice a few times a day, you can take control of your mind and choose the thoughts you want to have. It’s simple, but it takes practice—and it’s worth it
