## Caption

How to Experience Your Emotions Without Being Overwhelmed #emotions

## Transcript

Emotions are meant to be experienced. They're meant to have us feel something. It's a wonderful experience to have an emotion. It's part of the human condition. Emotions are meant to be felt, but they're not meant to be the boss. They're not meant to drive us and tell us what to do. And some emotions attempt to do that. Fear, for example. When we get scared, we also often feel compelled to take a certain action. That is not the time to take the action. That's the time to experience the fear, to embrace the fear, feel the fear, make sounds of the fear, draw a picture of the fear, tell a story about the fear, bask in the fear, but not let the fear tell you what to do or push you into doing something. Decisions need to be made by a combination of the heart and the head, but not by the emotions. When we let our emotions drive our behavior, we often get into trouble. We need to feel them, witness them and move on and make our decisions rationally with the heart in conjunction.
