Simple Strategy for Daily Self-Awareness

## Transcript

I'm an 85 year old clinical psychologist. One of the most important tools I can pass on to you is really very simple. It's taking a break during the day, maybe for 30 seconds, maybe it's a 60, maybe 120, two minutes, taking a break from whatever you're doing and just sitting down, getting steady and checking in with yourself the way you would with a good friend. If there aren't people around which you'd be self-conscious about, if you're alone, you might even talk to yourself out loud and say, hi, how you doing? How you feeling right now? What can you do to make yourself feel even more up, more positive? Have that conversation. If there are people around, you could have the conversation in your head. But just doing that, checking in with yourself, two, three, four, five, six, you name it, seven, eight times a day, you check in with yourself and then you take charge because you have options, because you're now more self-aware. Enjoy the experience.
