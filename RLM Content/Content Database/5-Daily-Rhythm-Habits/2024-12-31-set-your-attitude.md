## Caption

Your attitude isn’t random—it’s a choice.
Before you even get out of bed, ask yourself: What kind of attitude do I want to have today? Our attitude isn’t something that happens to us—it’s something we set and reset as needed.
Take a moment to check in with yourself throughout the day. Feeling off? Adjust your attitude. With practice and persistence, you can carry the energy you want wherever you go.
