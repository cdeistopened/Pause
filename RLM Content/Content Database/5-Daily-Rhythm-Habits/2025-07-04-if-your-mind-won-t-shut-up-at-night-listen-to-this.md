## Transcript

If you're bothered by thoughts trying to fall asleep, listen to this. I'm an 86-year-old clinical psychologist, and I'm here to tell you that the mind is a tool. It is not the boss. The mind is a tool for us to use. Most people think the mind is the boss because it constantly generates ideas, thoughts, thoughts, videos, but the mind isn't the boss. We can tell the mind what to think. We can tell the mind what kind of videos to produce, what kind of colors to show in our head. We cannot let it direct us. Otherwise we're going to be in big trouble. And one of the troubles is going to be anxiety. Anxiety often comes from the mind telling us about some terrible thing that's going to happen in the future. And we can't do anything about the future, but we think, oh, we're thinking of it, it must be important. No, because we're thinking something doesn't make it important. It just makes it something the mind generates.
