## Caption

New Year’s resolutions are promises for the future, but why wait? Real change can begin now.

## Transcript

New Year's resolutions are promises that are made not to be kept. New Year's resolutions are sort of stalling techniques. New Year's resolutions are a way of saying, I'm going to tell you now about something I'm going to do in the future, but that future may never come. Because if I say I'm going to stop drinking New Year's Day, that means I get to drink all Christmas. And then when New Year's Day comes, I'll come up with something like, well, why don't I wait till Super Bowl Saturday or some other event? And there's always something. The only way to start something is to start it now. And at the most, start it tomorrow morning with a specific time and a date. Anything else is baloney, pure baloney on a sandwich. If you want to start something, you start it right now. You want to start exercising, get up and exercise. Or start tomorrow morning at 9 o'clock, but no more than that in advance. Because otherwise, folks, that's just human nature. It will never happen.
