How to fix your bad mood

## Transcript

Most people I run into in my life think that the mood they're in during the day is a mood that somehow comes from within or comes from somewhere. I'm here to tell you that you get to choose your mood. You can be in any mood that you want, but you have to take the time to set the mood. And sometimes you have to stop during the day, check in on your mood and make adjustments. My advice is when you get up in the morning, before you do anything else, take a few minutes and decide on the mood that you want to have for the day. And then during the day, check in with yourself. Say to yourself, do I have that mood that I wanted to have? No. Well, how do I make some adjustments and get myself into that mood? Your mood is up to you. It's not something that comes from out of the sky. It's not something that bubbles up from within. It's something that you select. And when you do that, you get to choose any moods you want. And that is a wonderful gift to give yourself.
