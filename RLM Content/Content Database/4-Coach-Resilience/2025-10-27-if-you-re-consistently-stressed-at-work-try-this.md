## Transcript

I'm an 86 and a half year old clinical psychologist, and people are asking me what to do about stress at work. Take 60 seconds every half hour, 60 little seconds to just sit quietly with your eyes closed and breathe. That will take away a lot of the stress. Set your clock every half hour. If you don't want to do it every half hour, do it at least every hour. Take 60 seconds just to sit quietly with your eyes closed and breathe, and you will reduce your stress.
