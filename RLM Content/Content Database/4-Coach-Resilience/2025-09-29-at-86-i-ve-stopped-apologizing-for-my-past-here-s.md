## Caption

At 86, I've stopped apologizing for my past. Here's why you should too.

## Transcript

I'm an 86-year-old clinical psychologist. How do we let go of having regrets? A regret is about something that happened in the past. The most we can make from the past is when we look it over in terms of something we did and say to ourselves, how can I do that better next time? If we can learn how to do something better, there's some value in the past. Otherwise, forget about the past. We learn from them. and we do better. That's the best we can possibly do as people.
