## Caption

How I Overcame Injury with Persistence and Strength

## Transcript

I'm an 85-year-old clinical psychologist. Today I'm going to talk about the importance of persistence. Some years ago, I was severely damaged in a motorcycle accident. As a result, a couple of years ago, I started using a cane and a walking stick. And I needed them in order to ambulate. I couldn't get surgery because of the amount of metal that's in my leg that's in the way to correct my situation. And then it occurred to me, with the help of my daughter Serana, that if I built up muscles in my legs, they would compensate for the damage and for the lack of articulation of the knee. So I went on a weightlifting program. And I started lifting weights and strengthening my legs. It took 16 months of every week weightlifting. And then I was able to throw away the stick and the cane. Actually, I didn't throw them away. I put them away. But now I can walk without a stick or a cane. Why is it? Because every single week for 16 months, I lifted weights and worked on that strength. And that was what it takes, whether it's in weightlifting, whether it's in accomplishing other kinds of tasks. There are all kinds of things in this world that we can do if we plug at it every single week long enough until we master it in accomplishment. That is what persistence is about. And it really pays off.
