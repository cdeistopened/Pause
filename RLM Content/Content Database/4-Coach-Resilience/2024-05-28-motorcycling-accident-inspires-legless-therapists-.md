## Transcript

I took up an old hobby again, and the old hobby was motorcycling. And I was out there with the young people having a great time motorcycling. One day I was out with the gang, and there was a Winnebago motor vehicle coming at me on my side of the road, and he hit me head-on and ran over me and crushed both of my legs. I was there on the Blacktop Highway, and I tried to lift my left leg, and my foot came up and hit me in the face. I knew that it was no longer connected in some way. And so I closed my eyes, and I saw myself drifting out into the great beyond, way out into the blackness. And I knew I was dying, and I accepted it, and I let it go, and I started to drift way out. And then when I was way out in space, I saw a picture of myself strapped into a wheelchair with no legs, and I was sitting there with a patient, and I was doing my work in my office. And I heard a voice say, Richard, even without legs you can still make a contribution.
