Let's talk about anxiety. Hi, I'm Dr. Richard Lewis Miller. I'm an 86-year-old clinical psychologist. What is anxiety? It's an uncomfortable feeling in the chest that sometimes spreads out to the arms and the stomach, and it brings with it a feeling of imminent doom like something terrible is going to happen. The number one thing you can do about anxiety is to learn how to conscious breathe. Conscious breathing is an antidote to anxiety. What else can you do for anxiety? You can go for a walk. You can have a talk with a friend. You can distract yourself away from the anxiety. But focusing on it with the breathing is the number one antidote. Once you master conscious breathing, you will have your anxiety reduced significantly, if not get rid of it altogether.
