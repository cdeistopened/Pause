## Caption

Are your words empowering you—or making you a victim?
Our language shapes our reality. When you say, “Why does this always happen to me?” or “I never get it right,” you’re stepping into the role of a victim. The truth? Words like always and never rarely apply in life—they just hold us back.
Start listening to your inner dialogue. Are your words lifting you up or tearing you down? Replace critical thoughts with constructive ones and witness the shift in your mindset.
What’s one phrase you’ll swap for something more empowering today?
