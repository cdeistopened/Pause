## Transcript

Some of you may already know that my wife was recently diagnosed with breast cancer and she's going to soon have tests to find out whether it's metastasized or not. She's also going to have a lumpectomy. Many people when this happens to them get very scared because they're afraid of what's going to happen in the future. I'm going to get bad news. I'm going to find out something terrible. And what that does is it ruins their day because we really don't know what we're going to find out next week or in two weeks. So what's the point in ruining the day? We're not ruining our days. We're not living with catastrophic expectations of something terrible happening. We're living every day to the fullest, being here in the now. If something happens bad in the future, it'll happen in the future and we'll deal with it in the future. But right now, this is the time of our lives and we're doing everything we can to enjoy the time of our lives to the fullest. You might consider doing the same. Let go of your catastrophic expectations and enjoy the moment. Until next time, this is Dr. Richard Lewis Miller reminding you that good health is worth fighting for.
