## Caption

How to Live in the Now
Living in the now doesn't mean neglecting the future. It's about balancing the present moment with thoughtful preparation for what's ahead. Embrace today, but don't forget to plan for tomorrow—it's all part of the journey.

## Transcript

A friend of mine recently came to me and he said, you know, I've been practicing living in the now that you've been educating people about. I've been practicing that and yes, there's a lot of value in it, but you know, I didn't plan for my future and now I'm concerned at this age, I'm 75 years old, that I'm running out of money. What's going on here? Well, you need to clarify. There's a difference between living in the now and when never planning for the future. When you do plan for the future, that is a now experience, it's an intellectual experience. Living in the now is meant to be an emotional experience. How do I feel right now? What are my surroundings like? And being present with the people who are in front of you so when you're with them, you're not thinking about five other things. That's being in the now. Do you need to spend some of your now time planning for tomorrow in terms of events that may come? Yes, you do. Because like all bears, you've got to put away some meat so you've got to eat when you get back into the cave. That's just how life is. But if you worry about it and go into the future saying you're going to run out, then you're besmirching your now experience and we're going to talk about that another time.
