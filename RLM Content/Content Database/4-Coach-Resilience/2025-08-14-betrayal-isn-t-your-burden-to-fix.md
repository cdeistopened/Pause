## Caption

Betrayal isn't your burden to fix.

## Transcript

How do I rebuild trust after I've been betrayed? It's not for us to rebuild trust if someone has betrayed us, because we didn't do anything. It's the person who did the betrayal who has work to do. All we need to know is that we trust ourselves to not betray other people. So because one person betrayed you, that doesn't mean other people are going to betray you. That's just a way of telling you that, yes, there are people in the world who will betray us. But that doesn't mean we have to walk around feeling untrusting of of the entire world, because yes, there are a small percentage of people who lie. There's a small percentage of people who betray. There are a small percentage of people who commit crimes. We needn't walk around in the world thinking every single person is a crime doer or a murderer or a betrayer. We can walk in the world knowing that we ourselves are going to maintain our own standards. That's the key.
