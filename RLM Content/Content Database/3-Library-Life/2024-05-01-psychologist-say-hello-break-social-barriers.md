## Caption

The Power of Hello
Let’s make our world a friendlier place—one hello at a time.

## Transcript

I'm an 85-year-old clinical psychologist, and I've been helping people for 64 years. When I was growing up as a little boy in New York, I would go up and down on the elevator because my folks lived in an apartment, which I lived in with them. People never spoke to each other in the elevator. People didn't speak to each other in the theater. I would sit down in the theater with my folks. The person sitting next to me would never say hello. I found that to be true all my life. How many places do we all go in public where we're sitting inches away from another person and we never say hello to them? When I say to all of that, enough already. Let's start saying hello to one another. In the United States, which is divided, let us unite by simply saying hello. Say hello to the checker in the supermarket. Say hello to the retail clerk in the store you go to. Say hello to the plumber in the plumbing store. Introduce yourself. Hi, my name's Richard. Glad to meet you. Let's break down all this anonymity and this distance and really be the tribal people who we are and say at least hello. How are you doing? A little human contact as we go through our daily work. Try it out for three months. See what happens when you start saying hello and introducing yourselves to people.
