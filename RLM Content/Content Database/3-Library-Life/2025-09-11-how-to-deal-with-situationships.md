Here's a question made me tickle a little bit. The person says, how do I navigate relationships or people giving me mixed signals? Two things come to mind. One is, you'll be very honest with them. You say, I don't understand what you're saying. Could you clarify? I'm confused by some of the things you just said. Maybe you could elaborate. Get the person who is giving mixed signals to elaborate, to clarify, to give you more information. What you don't want to do is point the finger at them and say, hey, you're giving me mixed signals because that's accusatory. It's going to make them feel bad. They're going to get defensive. You're not going to get a great answer. But if you become the... acquire a person, the person who's curious, who wants to know more, then they're more likely to give you more information and hopefully enough so you get a straight signal.
