## Caption

Do you feel confident asking your doctor the tough questions?
Our relationship with doctors is critical, yet many of us hesitate to speak up. You have every right to ask: Is this the best treatment? Are there alternatives? What side effects can I expect? If you’re unsure about speaking up, bring an advocate with you—it’s your health, and it’s worth fighting for.
