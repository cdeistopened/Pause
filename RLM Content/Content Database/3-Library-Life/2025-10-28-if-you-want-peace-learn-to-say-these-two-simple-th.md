## Caption

If you want peace, learn to say these two simple things.

## Transcript

I'm an 86-year-old clinical psychologist, and the key to graceful living, to harmonious living is apology and forgiveness. I'm sorry I hurt your feelings. I'm sorry that I spoke to you in a condescending way. Whatever it happens to be, the person who did the act that hurt the other person must say I am sorry. And then it's up to the other person to immediately say, I forgive you. Thank you for apologizing. I forgive you, it's okay. And then you either shake hands or hug and kiss, and you move on with a graceful life. And that's the end. And that's the beauty of repair.
