## Transcript

People ask me about getting into the habit of saying yes and not being able to say no. This is a rather dangerous habit to have because there are many times for all of us when we want to say no. We simply do not want what's happening or what's being offered or what's being asked for. And yet, if we have this compulsion to say yes, if we've been habituated to saying yes, it can be difficult to say no. And so the key here is to practice. It's like so many other areas of communication. It's skill training and practice. You want to practice saying no. Get together with friends. Have them ask you something and practice saying no. You do a little play acting and you practice it over and over again. And they practice it in different situations. See if you can come up with different little plays with your friends. Make a game out of it, but practice saying no so that at any time in your life when there's a choice between yes and no, you'll have a real choice to say yes or no.
