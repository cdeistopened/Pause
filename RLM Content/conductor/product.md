# Product Goals

This project, "PAUSE," is a multi-platform content initiative centered around the life's work of Dr. Richard Miller, an 86-year-old psychologist with 65 years of clinical experience. The core mission is to "open source" his wisdom for managing mental and emotional well-being.

The project has two primary components:

1.  **The Book: "PAUSE: Why Therapy Fails and Tactical Habits Save You"**
    *   This is a static, "snap-action" manual that details Dr. Miller's core protocols for mental and emotional regulation.
    *   It covers foundational techniques such as breathing, counting, witnessing, and imagery, which form "The PAUSE System."
    *   The book also includes a "Troubleshooting Library" with specific applications for issues like grief, addiction, conflict, and anxiety.

2.  **The App: "The Digital Mentor"**
    *   This is a dynamic, AI-powered companion to the book.
    *   It uses "Accumulated Intelligence Technology" (a large language model trained on Dr. Miller's work) to provide on-demand coaching in his voice.
    *   The app is designed to help users practice and "saturate" the PAUSE protocols in their daily lives, moving from theory to application.

The overall vision is to create a comprehensive ecosystem for mental wellness, combining the timeless wisdom of a seasoned practitioner with the accessibility and interactivity of modern AI technology. The target audience is anyone seeking practical, actionable tools to improve their mental and emotional health outside of traditional therapy models.
