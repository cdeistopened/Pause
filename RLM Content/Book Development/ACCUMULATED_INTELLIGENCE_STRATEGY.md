# STRATEGY: Accumulated Intelligence (AI)

## The Core Thesis
There are two forms of "AI" in this project:
1.  **Human AI (Accumulated Intelligence)**: The 86-year-old brain of Dr. Richard Miller, which has processed 65 years of clinical practice and a lifetime of high-stakes trauma. This is "intelligence" that has been *accumulated* and refined into a set of simple, effective protocols.
2.  **Technological AI (Accumulated Intelligence Technology)**: The modern large language model (LLM) that has been trained on Richard's "Human AI" dataset (books, transcripts, videos).

## The Book vs. The App
*   **The Book**: A static "Snap-Action" manual. It is the core framework. It is the training manual for the muscle.
*   **The App**: The dynamic "Companion." Because a book cannot answer back, the App uses AI to provide "Richard-on-demand."
    *   *Dynamic Response*: The user can ask "I'm in a crisis, what do I do?" and the App responds using the protocols in Richard's voice.
    *   *Saturation Support*: The App triggers the "Micro-Doses" (ALOT) throughout the day, ensuring the reader moves from *reading about* the Pause to *building the muscle* of the Pause.

## Strategic Integration
*   The Book will mention the App in the **Foreword**, the **Intro to Part II**, and the **Conclusion**.
*   We frame the App as an extension of Richard's "Why": he wants to be an "Influencer" because he wants to "Open Source" his intelligence for the pursuit of happiness. The App is the most efficient delivery vehicle for that open-source intelligence.
