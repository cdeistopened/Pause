*   **The Event**: The Winnebago crosses the center line. The impact.
*   **The Detail**: "I watched in horror as my lower leg, no longer properly connected to my upper leg, swung up and hit me in the face."

## Part 2: The Problem (The Hijacking)
**Objective**: Show the reader that they are in the same crash, just in slow motion.

*   **The Pivot**: You don't need to be hit by a Winnebago to experience shock. You are experiencing it right now.
*   **The Symptom**: Waking up to mental chatter. The 3 AM worry loop. The inability to be present with your partner because you are "somewhere else."
*   **The Villain**: The "Drifting Mind" (The Hijacker).
*   **The Reality**: "Your mind is generating thoughts about the thoughts it’s generating." It thinks it is the Boss. You are the passenger.

## Part 3: The Agitation (The Failure of Dilution)
**Objective**: Destroy the reader's faith in "Slow Therapy" and "Trying Harder."

*   **The Math of Failure**:
    *   1 hour of therapy = 50 minutes.
    *   The rest of the week = 6 days, 23 hours.
    *   **Ratio**: 1 vs. 167. This is **Dilution**.
*   **The Consequences**: You cannot fix a habit of 10,000 negative thoughts a day with one hour of talking a week.
*   **The Pain**: This is why you feel stuck. This is why you read self-help books and nothing changes. You are fighting a flood with a bucket.

**Objective**: Introduce the **PAUSE System** as the inevitable answer.

*   **The Concept**: **Saturation**.
*   **The Methodology**: "A Little Over Time is a Lot" (ALOT).
*   **The Promise**: 10 micro-doses a day x 365 days = 3,650 neural corrections.
*   **The Mechanism**: We don't fight the mind; we **Pause** it. We jam the signal. We re-record over the old tape.
