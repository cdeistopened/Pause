# The "Badass" Pivot: Strategic Overhaul

## The "Breaking Bad" Archetype (Mike Ehrmantraut)
Dr. Miller isn't a guru; he's a "cleaner." He’s the guy you call when the situation is FUBAR. He’s 6'5", bald, 86 years old, and he’s been run over by a recreational vehicle and lived to tell the tale.
*   **The Vibe**: No-nonsense, highly competent, slightly intimidating but deeply protective. "I'm not here to hold your hand; I'm here to stop the bleeding."

## The Pivot: "Internal Chemistry" (The Micro-Dose Angle)
*   **The Backstory Hook**: Richard has spent 50 years studying *external* psychedelics (suppressed, illegal, powerful).
*   **The Twist**: Because they took the medicine away, he had to engineer the same effects using *internal* chemistry.
*   **The Promise**: You can "micro-dose" sanity/serotonin/dopamine using your own biology, instantly, legally, and for free. He is the "Pharmacist" of the internal dashboard.

## The New Villain: The "Hijacker"
The villain isn't just "intrusive thoughts"; it's **The Hijacker**. 
*   The Hijacker is the primitive part of your brain that takes the wheel of the Ferrari and drives it into a ditch. 
*   The Hijacker writes "Horror Movies" about the future.
*   The Hijacker replays "Old Tapes" of the past.
*   **The Goal**: Neutralize the Hijacker. Regaining "Command Authority."

## Structure: The "Pharmacist's Manual"
Instead of "The Pause," the chapters are "Prescriptions" or "Protocols."
*   **Protocol 1: The Airway (Breathing)** -> Instead of "Anxiety Antidote," it's "Stabilize the System."
*   **Protocol 2: The Displacement (Counting)** -> "Jamming the Signal."
*   **Protocol 3: The Witness (Observation)** -> "Intelligence Gathering."
*   **Protocol 4: The Override (Imagery)** -> "Injecting the Antidote."

This feels much more "Airport Bestseller." It’s aggressive, it has a secret (internal drugs), and it attacks the status quo (therapy is too slow).
